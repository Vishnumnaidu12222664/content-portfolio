## ⚡ Quick Customization Guide

### 1. Adding Your Personal Photograph
- Place your personal photo into `assets/images/` and name it `vishnum-photo.jpg`.
- The hero section automatically displays your photo.

### 2. Updating Your Resume PDF
- The website is already linked to `Vishnum_Naidu_Resume.pdf`.
- Whenever you have an updated PDF, simply replace `Vishnum_Naidu_Resume.pdf` in the root folder with your file. Both the top navigation "Resume" button and the hero "Download Resume" button will immediately download it.

### 3. Adding Your Live Social & Project Links
Open `index.html` and search for `yourusername` or `yourchannel` to paste your real URLs:
- **Instagram**: replace `https://instagram.com/yourusername`
- **YouTube**: replace `https://youtube.com/@yourchannel`
- **LinkedIn**: replace `https://linkedin.com/in/yourprofile`
- **Project Cards**: in the `#work` section, update the `href` in each project card to your live YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, or case studies.
